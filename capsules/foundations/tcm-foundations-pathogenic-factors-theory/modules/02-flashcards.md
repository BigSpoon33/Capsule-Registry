## What are the primary characteristics of wind as a pathogenic factor?
?
Wind is a highly mobile and dynamic pathogenic factor, characterized by rapid onset, causing sudden and unpredictable symptoms.

---

## How does external wind primarily affect the body?
?
External wind primarily affects the top part of the body, particularly the Lungs, and can manifest as Wind-Cold, Wind-Heat, Wind-Dampness, Wind-Dryness, and Wind-Water.

---

## What are the symptoms of Wind-Cold?
?
Wind-Cold causes chills, fever, and cough.

---

## What are the symptoms of Wind-Heat?
?
Wind-Heat causes fever, thirst, and redness in the face.

---

## What are the symptoms of Wind-Dampness?
?
Wind-Dampness causes body aches and joint pain.

---

## What are the symptoms of Wind-Dryness?
?
Wind-Dryness leads to dryness and itching.

---

## How does external wind affect the face?
?
Wind can cause paralysis or stiffness in the facial muscles.

---

## How does external wind affect the channels and joints?
?
Wind can cause pain and obstruction in the channels and joints.

---

## How can wind affect the Liver channel?
?
Wind can affect the Liver, causing symptoms like dizziness and tremors.

---

## What is internal wind associated with, and what causes it?
?
Internal Wind is associated with liver disorders, often caused by emotional factors or liver yin deficiency.
