## 1. Differentiating External and Internal Wind

**Question:**
How would you differentiate between external and internal wind pathogenic factors in a clinical scenario?

**Key Concepts to Cover:**
- **External Wind**:
  - Symptoms of invasion in the Lung’s Defensive Qi portion.
  - Symptoms of affecting the channels and joints.
- **Internal Wind**:
  - Symptoms related to liver disorders.
  - Symptoms associated with emotional factors or liver yin deficiency.

**Assessment Criteria:**
- The ability to identify the primary characteristics of external and internal wind.
- The ability to differentiate between symptoms caused by external and internal wind.
- The ability to understand the underlying causes of internal wind.

**Reference Content:**
External Wind is characterized by rapid onset, causing sudden and unpredictable symptoms, while Internal Wind is associated with liver disorders, often caused by emotional factors or liver yin deficiency.

## 2. Identifying Cold Invasions

**Question:**
A patient presents with chills, fever, and joint pain. How would you differentiate between external cold and wind-cold based on their clinical presentation?

**Key Concepts to Cover:**
- **External Cold**:
  - Symptoms of invasion in the Lung’s Defensive Qi portion.
  - Symptoms of affecting the channels and joints.
- **Wind-Cold**:
  - Symptoms of rapid onset and sudden onset of chills and fever.
  - Symptoms of causing cough.

**Assessment Criteria:**
- The ability to distinguish between the primary symptoms of external cold and wind-cold.
- The ability to recognize the presence of wind in the clinical presentation.
- The ability to identify the specific manifestations of cold invasion.

**Reference Content:**
External Cold primarily affects the Kidneys and can cause clear discharges, while Wind-Cold is characterized by rapid onset and sudden onset of chills and fever, leading to cough.

## 3. Differentiating Summer-Heat from Dampness

**Question:**
A patient presents with thirst, restlessness, and joint pain. How would you differentiate between summer-heat and dampness based on their clinical presentation?

**Key Concepts to Cover:**
- **Summer-Heat**:
  - Symptoms of injuring Yin.
  - Symptoms of being scattering and causing sweating.
- **Dampness**:
  - Symptoms of causing body aches and joint pain.
  - Symptoms of leading to edema and swelling.

**Assessment Criteria:**
- The ability to recognize the primary symptoms of summer-heat and dampness.
- The ability to differentiate between the scattering nature of summer-heat and the more localized nature of dampness.
- The ability to identify the underlying cause of joint pain.

**Reference Content:**
Summer-Heat can lead to symptoms like thirst, restlessness, and irritability, while dampness can cause body aches and joint pain, and lead to edema and swelling.
