## What are the clinical manifestations associated with the Governing Vessel (Du Mai)?
?
Symptoms such as fever, headache, dizziness, and mental agitation are associated with the Governing Vessel.

---

## Which acupuncture points are commonly used to treat the Governing Vessel (Du Mai)?
?
Points such as Du16 (Du Shu) and Du20 (Du Wei) are commonly used for the Governing Vessel.

---

## Which herbs are used to tonify the brain and nourish the marrow when treating the Governing Vessel (Du Mai)?
?
Herbs like Gou Teng (Uncaria rhynchophylla) and Bai Zi Ren (Semen Ziziphi Spinosae) are used to tonify the brain and nourish the marrow.

---

## Which herbs are used to clear heat and tonify yin when treating the Governing Vessel (Du Mai)?
?
Herbs such as Zhi Mu (Anemarrhena asphodeloides) and Sheng Di Huang (Rehmannia glutinosa) are used to clear heat and tonify yin.

---

## What are the clinical manifestations associated with the Directing Vessel (Ren Mai)?
?
Symptoms like gynecological disorders, lower back pain, and infertility are associated with the Directing Vessel.

---

## Which acupuncture points are commonly used to treat the Directing Vessel (Ren Mai)?
?
Points such as Ren4 (Guanyuan) and Ren6 (Qihai) are often used for the Directing Vessel.

---

## Which herbs are used to nourish the uterus and blood when treating the Directing Vessel (Ren Mai)?
?
Herbs like Dang Gui (Angelica sinensis) and Bai Shao (Paeonia lactiflora) are used to nourish the uterus and blood.

---

## Which herbs are used to nourish yin and clear empty-heat when treating the Directing Vessel (Ren Mai)?
?
Herbs such as Sheng Di Huang (Rehmannia glutinosa) and Mai Men Dong (Ophiopogon japonicus) are used to nourish yin and clear empty-heat.

---

## What are the clinical manifestations associated with the Penetrating Vessel (Chong Mai)?
?
Symptoms such as menstrual disorders, uterine bleeding, and lower abdominal pain are associated with the Penetrating Vessel.

---

## Which herbs are used to tonify the uterus when treating the Penetrating Vessel (Chong Mai)?
?
Herbs like Dang Gui (Angelica sinensis) and Wu Wei Zi (Schisandra chinensis) are used to tonify the uterus.
