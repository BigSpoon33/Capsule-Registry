## What is the name of the book where the Six Stage Theory is formulated?
?
Zhang Zhong Jing's "Discussion of Cold-Induced Diseases" (Shang Han Lun).

---

## Which two primary patterns are within the Greater Yang stage?
?
Invasion of Wind-Cold with Prevalence of Wind and Invasion of Wind-Cold with Prevalence of Cold.

---

## What are the clinical manifestations of the pattern of Invasion of Wind-Cold with Prevalence of Wind?
?
Aversion to cold, headache, stiff neck, floating pulse.

---

## What are the clinical manifestations of the pattern of Invasion of Wind-Cold with Prevalence of Cold?
?
Similar to the pattern of Invasion of Wind-Cold with Prevalence of Wind, but with a stronger emphasis on cold symptoms.

---

## Which acupuncture points are recommended for treating the pattern of Invasion of Wind-Cold with Prevalence of Wind?
?
Fengchi (GB20) and Baihui (GV20).

---

## Which acupuncture points are recommended for treating the pattern of Invasion of Wind-Cold with Prevalence of Cold?
?
Fengmen (BL12) and Weishu (BL21).

---

## Which herbal formula is used to dispel Wind-Cold in the pattern of Invasion of Wind-Cold with Prevalence of Wind?
?
Gui Zhi Tang (Cinnamon Twig Decoction).

---

## Which herbal formula is used to dispel Wind-Cold in the pattern of Invasion of Wind-Cold with Prevalence of Cold?
?
Ma Huang Tang (Ephedra Decoction).

---

## What are the clinical manifestations of the pattern of Accumulation of Water?
?
Edema, swelling, and discomfort.

---

## Which acupuncture points are recommended for treating the pattern of Accumulation of Water?
?
Yinlingquan (SP9) and Zusanli (ST36).
