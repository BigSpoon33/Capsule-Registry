## What are the three burners in TCM and their corresponding locations?
?
The three burners in TCM are the Upper Burner (Heart and Lungs), Middle Burner (Stomach and Spleen), and Lower Burner (Kidneys and Bladder).

---

## What are the clinical manifestations of Wind-Heat in the Lungs’ Defensive Qi portion?
?
The clinical manifestations include fever, aversion to cold, headache, sore throat, slight sweating, runny nose with yellow discharge, swollen tonsils, body aches, slight thirst, a red tongue in the front or sides with a thin white coating, and a floating and rapid pulse.

---

## Which formula is the first choice for treating the beginning stage of invasions of Wind-Heat according to the Upper Burner pattern?
?
Lonicera–Forsythia Powder (Yin Qiao San) is the formula of choice for the beginning stage of invasions of Wind-Heat.

---

## What are the clinical manifestations of Heat in the Qi level of the Lungs?
?
The clinical manifestations include persistent fever, severe headache, dry cough, yellow and sticky phlegm, thirst, a red tongue with a yellow coating, and a slippery and rapid pulse.

---

## What is the pathology of Heat in the Qi level of the Lungs?
?
The Qi level of the Lungs is invaded by Heat, leading to symptoms of coughing and phlegm production.

---

## Which herbal formula is commonly used to treat Heat in the Qi level of the Lungs?
?
Mahuang Xingren Ganshi Decoction (Ma Xing Shi Gan Tang) is commonly used to treat Heat in the Qi level of the Lungs.

---

## What are the clinical manifestations of Heat in the Pericardium (Nutritive Qi level)?
?
The clinical manifestations include high fever, severe headache, delirium, confusion, a red and dry tongue, and a thin and rapid pulse.

---

## Which formula is the first choice for treating Heat in the Pericardium?
?
Qingying Decoction (Qing Ying Tang) is the formula of choice for treating Heat in the Pericardium.

---

## What are the clinical manifestations of Heat in the Middle Burner (Bright Yang)?
?
The clinical manifestations include fever, thirst, restlessness, a red and dry tongue, and a thin and rapid pulse.

---

## Which acupuncture points are recommended for treating Heat in the Bright Yang?
?
Acupuncture points that clear Heat and regulate the Middle Burner are recommended for treating Heat in the Bright Yang.
