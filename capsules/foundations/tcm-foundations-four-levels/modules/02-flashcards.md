## What are warm diseases characterized by?
?
Warm diseases are characterized by heat symptoms and are considered external pathogenic factors that invade the body.

---

## Which two pathogenic factors combine to form Wind-Heat?
?
Wind and heat pathogenic factors combine to form Wind-Heat.

---

## At which level do warm diseases first appear according to the Four Levels Theory?
?
Warm diseases first appear at the Defensive Qi (Wei) level.

---

## What are the symptoms associated with the Qi level of the Four Levels Theory?
?
Symptoms at the Qi level include coughing, thirst, and changes in appetite.

---

## Which level of the Four Levels Theory is associated with petechiae and hemorrhagic rashes?
?
The Blood Level (Xue) is associated with petechiae and hemorrhagic rashes.

---

## What are the four levels of the Four Levels Theory?
?
The four levels are the Defensive Qi (Wei), Qi, Nutritive Qi (Ying), and Blood (Xue) levels.

---

## Which level corresponds to the skin and muscle layer?
?
The Defensive Qi (Wei) level corresponds to the skin and muscle layer.

---

## What are the symptoms of the Defensive Qi (Wei) level?
?
Symptoms of the Defensive Qi (Wei) level include chills, fever, and sweating.

---

## What type of rash is commonly seen in milder cases of warm diseases?
?
Maculopapular rashes are commonly seen in milder cases of warm diseases.

---

## What is the deepest level of the Four Levels Theory?
?
The Blood Level (Xue) is the deepest level of the Four Levels Theory.
